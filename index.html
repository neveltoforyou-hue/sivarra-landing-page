The House of Sivarra

Beauty, as ritual.

Sivarra is a sanctuary for those who treat beauty as ritual. Situated in the heart of Ashiyana, our atelier brings master hair artisans, skin aestheticians, makeup artists, and spa therapists under a single, considered roof.

No spectacle, no templates — only patient, generous craft. Our chairs welcome him and her with equal reverence, because luxury, to us, is intimacy.
